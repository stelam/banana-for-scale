How Many Bananas is 5'11"?

5 feet 11 inches equals approximately 10.14 bananas. This measurement uses the standard banana length of 17.78 centimeters (about 7 inches), which is the average size of a medium Cavendish banana.

The Math Behind It

To convert height to bananas:

  • 5 feet 11 inches = 180.34 centimeters
  • 180.34 cm ÷ 17.78 cm (banana length) = 10.14 bananas

Why Measure in Bananas?

The "banana for scale" concept started as an internet phenomenon where people would include bananas in photos to give viewers a sense of an object's size. It's since become a fun, relatable way to understand measurements.
