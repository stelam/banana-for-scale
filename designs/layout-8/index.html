Understanding Banana Measurements

The "banana for scale" phenomenon started as an internet meme but has evolved into a genuinely useful way to understand measurements. Our converter uses the standard banana length of 17.78 cm (7 inches) — the average size of a medium Cavendish banana.

Why 17.78 cm?

The Cavendish banana, which makes up about 47% of global banana production, typically ranges from 15-20 cm when ripe. We use 17.78 cm as it represents the median of commercially sold bananas in major markets.

Common Conversions

  • Average human height (5'7"): ~9.6 bananas
  • Standard door height (6'8"): ~11.4 bananas
  • Basketball hoop (10 feet): ~17.1 bananas
  • Olympic swimming pool (50m): ~281 bananas

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the banana measurement?

Our converter uses 17.78 cm as the standard banana length. While individual bananas vary, this measurement represents the average commercial banana size and provides consistent, comparable results.

Can I use this for weight conversions too?

Yes! We also support weight conversions using the average banana weight of 118 grams. Switch to "Weight" mode to convert between grams, kilograms, pounds, and bananas.

Where did "banana for scale" come from?

The phrase originated on image-sharing platforms where users would place bananas next to objects to show their size. It became a viral meme and is now widely recognized as a humorous but effective scale reference.
